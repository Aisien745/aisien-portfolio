## Key Strategic Takeaways

- **Tesla's Moat:** Brand premium and software ecosystem.
- **BYD's Advantage:** Vertical integration (they make their own batteries) and aggressive pricing.

## Frameworks Applied

### 1. Porter's Five Forces
- **Threat of New Entrants:** High in China due to government subsidies (NIO, XPeng).
- **Supplier Power:** Low for BYD (vertical integration), Medium for Tesla.

### 2. Unit Economics
BYD achieves lower cost-per-vehicle by controlling the entire supply chain, allowing them to price aggressively in the mass market while Tesla defends the premium segment.
