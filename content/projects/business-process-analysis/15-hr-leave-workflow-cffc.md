## 01. Modeling Scope & Notation Selection

To eliminate informal verbal approvals and ambiguous handoffs across administrative departments at **Cherepovetskie Paints**, the internal employee leave approval workflow was formally modeled for **SOP Standardization (*Регламентация*)**.

* **Client Organization:** Cherepovetskie Paints
* **Modeling Objective:** Process Standardization & Regulatory Documentation
* **Notation Standard:** Cross-Functional Flowchart (**CFFC**) — chosen to map sequential task execution, role ownership boundaries (*Employee*, *HR Department*, *Manager*), and formal approval decision gates in a single visual standard.

## 02. Cross-Functional Process Architecture (CFFC)

![Employee Leave Application & Approval CFFC Flowchart](/CFFC.jpg)

### End-to-End Stage & Decision Gate Breakdown

| Stage | Process Step | Accountable Role | Gate Logic & Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Leave Initiation** | Employee | Triggered at process start when an employee requests scheduled leave. |
| **02** | **Application Submission** | Employee | Employee completes the standardized leave request form and submits it to HR. |
| **03** | **HR Compliance Review** | HR Department | HR verifies accrued leave balance, statutory notice rules, and documentation accuracy. |
| **04** | **HR Decision Point** | HR Department | **Approved:** Advances to line manager. **Rejected:** Returned to employee for correction. |
| **05** | **Managerial Schedule Review** | Manager | Department manager evaluates shift coverage and approves requested leave dates. |
| **06** | **Manager Decision Point** | Manager | **Approved:** Advances to final sign-off. **Rejected:** Returned to employee. |
| **07** | **Final Closure** | HR / System | Leave application is marked complete upon securing all required approvals. |

## 03. Process Optimization & Automation Roadmap

To transition the standardized CFFC workflow from manual paper routing into a scalable digital process, three targeted improvements were proposed:

1. **Automated Status Notifications:** Implement automated portal and email alerts to notify employees immediately upon HR or managerial approval, rejection, or revision requests—eliminating manual status follow-ups.
2. **Electronic Signature (e-Signature) Adoption:** Enable legally binding digital sign-offs for employees, HR specialists, and department managers to compress approval cycle times and eliminate physical document handoffs.
3. **Direct HRIS Integration:** Integrate the leave request portal with the core Human Resource Information System (HRIS) so approved leave dates automatically update shift rosters, attendance logs, and payroll calculations without duplicate data entry.
