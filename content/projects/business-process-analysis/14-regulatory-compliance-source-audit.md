## 01. Audit Scope & Information Hygiene Methodology

When modeling internal HR and administrative workflows at **Cherepovetskie Paints**, relying on unverified secondary summaries introduces acute legal and financial exposure. To ensure internal Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) reflect binding statutory requirements for **Organizational Military Registration (*Воинский учёт*)**, primary regulatory sources were validated, partially obsolete guidance was quarantined, and operational compliance rules were codified for HR execution.

## 04. Codified HR Compliance & Staffing Rules

### 1. Three Statutory Categories of Military Registration
To segment employee records accurately within HR databases, personnel must be classified across three distinct registration tiers:
* **General Registration (*Общий учёт*):** Standard record-keeping covering all eligible employees liable for military service without special exemptions.
* **Special Registration (*Специальный учёт*):** Dedicated tracking for critical technical and production personnel granted mobilization deferral (*бронирование*) to preserve industrial continuity.
* **Municipal / Place-of-Residence Registration (*Учёт по месту жительства*):** Local self-government registries maintained at the municipal level to account for all liable residents within a territory.

### 2. Sizing Dedicated HR Compliance Headcount
Resource allocation for maintaining statutory registries is governed by three workload drivers:
* **Eligible Headcount:** Total number of employees on payroll subject to military registration.
* **Administrative Volume:** Frequency of hiring, role transfers, address changes, and statutory notifications.
* **Document-Turnover Complexity:** Scale of internal register maintenance and external commissariat reconciliations.

### 3. Regulatory Enforcement & Reporting Updates
* **Heightened Penalty Exposure:** Expanded administrative fines for missed filing deadlines, incomplete registries, or failure to notify authorities of employee status changes.
* **Expanded Employer Reporting Duties:** Mandatory submission of updated employee personal, educational, and role data within compressed statutory windows.
* **Updated Forms & Filing Workflows:** Revised standardized reporting templates requiring immediate updates to internal HR onboarding and offboarding checklists.
