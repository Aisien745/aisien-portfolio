## 01. Change Driver & Management Level Classification

Before deploying updated regulations across **Cherepovetskie Paints**, the organizational change initiative was classified by its underlying trigger (**Change Driver**) and decision-making tier (**Management Level**) to calibrate governance and measurement:

* **Change Driver — Abnormal (Reactive):** The transformation is driven by acute operational friction—outdated operating manuals and missing process descriptions that cause recurring shop-floor errors. Because these changes respond to systemic breakdowns rather than a pre-scheduled growth cycle, rapid corrective stabilization takes priority.
* **Management Level — Operational:** Interventions target day-to-day shop-floor execution, workstation instructions, and role-level compliance, directly reshaping how frontline operators and shift supervisors perform core production tasks.

## 02. Evaluation Metrics Architecture

To measure both **documentation rollout** and **behavioral impact** on the shop floor, core indicators extracted from the project brief were paired with three supplemental organizational health metrics:

### Core Rollout & Compliance Metrics
1. **Total SOP Coverage (`%`):** Share of core production and warehouse processes governed by updated, approved Standard Operating Procedures.
2. **Role-Specific Instruction Coverage (`Units`):** Number of completed workstation instructions mapped against total plant job roles.
3. **Post-Implementation Error Count (`Incidents`):** Frequency of recurring operational mistakes after SOP rollout compared against the pre-change baseline.

### Proposed Supplemental Impact Metrics
1. **New-Hire Onboarding Duration (`Days`):** Time required for newly hired operators to reach independent shift productivity before vs. after SOP standardization.
2. **Employee Process Satisfaction (`% / Survey Score`):** Frontline clarity and usability score captured via structured shop-floor pulse surveys.
3. **Production Plan Attainment Rate (`%`):** Percentage of scheduled shift and monthly output targets met on time following regulatory updates.

## 04. Executive Diagnostic & Corrective Priorities

1. **Close the Error-Rate Gap (`Red` Indicator):** While 100% of staff signed off on updated SOPs and 95% of processes are documented, post-rollout errors reached **15 incidents** against a ceiling of **10**. Formal acknowledgment alone does not guarantee behavioral compliance on the shop floor.
2. **Deliver the Deferred 5th Training Workshop (`Amber` Indicator):** Completing the remaining hands-on shift training session and finalizing the final **5 role-specific instructions** will directly address the execution gap driving the error overrun.
