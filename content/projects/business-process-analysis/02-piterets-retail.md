## 01. Overview & Operational Context

The **Piterets** retail chain faces classic high-volume supermarket bottlenecks at the checkout interface. Evaluating the network involves examining:
* **Checkout Process Mapping (A16):** Visualizing the step-by-step transaction flow involving customer arrival, barcode scanning, cash/card payment processing, receipt generation, and purchase completion managed by cashiers and head cashiers.
* **Problem Field Evaluation:** Pinpointing root causes of financial leakage, customer churn, and resource misallocation.
* **Organizational Change Framework:** Structuring targeted interventions to optimize staff scheduling and queue length.

---

## 02. Key Problem Field Breakdown

### 1. Financial Inefficiencies
* **Inefficient Capital Allocation:** Financial losses driven by mismanaged labor scheduling, resulting in idle payroll expenses during low-traffic hours.
* **Lost Revenue (Abandoned Carts):** Customers abandoning full baskets due to excessive checkout friction and long waiting times.

### 2. Market Impact
* **Customer Churn via Long Queues:** Direct loss of customer lifetime value as shoppers defect to competitors with faster checkout times.
* **Import Sanction Pressures:** Restrictions affecting specific product categories and retail supply chain agility.

### 3. Process Gaps
* **Lack of Precise Resource Modeling:** Inability to accurately forecast staffing requirements against variable customer footfall.
* **Fragmented Information Systems:** Absence of a centralized knowledge base for standard operating procedures, complicating process audits.

### 4. Human Resources
* **High Staff Turnover:** Burnout and dissatisfaction driven by erratic scheduling and stressful peak-hour rushes.

---

## 03. Strategic Prioritization & Action Plan

To stabilize operations, leadership must target four primary problem areas:

1. **Inefficient Resource Allocation & Idle Payroll Costs**
   * *Justification:* Directly damages operating margins through bloated labor costs during off-peak windows.
2. **Customer Loss Due to Queues**
   * *Justification:* Represents direct top-line revenue destruction and long-term erosion of market share.
3. **Lack of Accurate Resource Calculation**
   * *Justification:* Prevents proactive workforce planning, causing compounding scheduling errors across retail units.
4. **Revenue Leakage (Abandoned Baskets)**
   * *Justification:* Signals uncaptured demand and degraded in-store customer experience.

### Implementation Objectives
* **Target Cashier Workload:** Maintain at least **85% utilization** efficiency.
* **Queue Length Constraint:** Cap peak queue depth at a maximum of **3 customers** per active register through dynamic shift scheduling and process simulation modeling.
