## 01. Resource Taxonomy & Budgeting Methodology

Executing process changes requires mapping every operational input into structured resource categories before estimating capital requirements:
* **Temporary Resources:** Time-bound specialist labor allocated to implementation tasks (calculated via hourly rates based on standard 160–173.3 working hours/month).
* **Permanent Resources:** Ongoing operational headcount required to sustain day-to-day execution post-launch.
* **Material Resources:** Divided into **Governing** (software, algorithms, and control systems), **Consumable** (raw materials, uniforms, and printouts), and **Supporting** (real estate, renovations, hardware, and marketing infrastructure).

## 02. Client 1: Casual Coffee Shop (Second Location Launch)

### Resource Classification
* **Temporary:** Senior barista staff training (40 hrs); operating SOP development (120 hrs total: 20 hrs senior barista + 100 hrs business analyst).
* **Permanent:** Frontline baristas (2 FTE @ 60,000 RUB/mo); location manager (1 FTE @ 75,000 RUB/mo).
* **Material (Consumables):** Coffee beans (30,000 RUB); operational consumables (45,000 RUB).
* **Material (Supporting):** Premises rent & utilities (70,000 RUB); interior renovation (150,000 RUB); online POS terminal (50,000 RUB); launch advertising (80,000 RUB); leased coffee equipment (0 RUB).

### Launch Budget & Financial Projection

| Budget Line Item | Category | Calculation / Basis | Cost (RUB) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | ---: |
| **Staff Training (Senior Barista)** | Temporary | 40 hrs allocation (70,000 RUB/mo base) | 70,000 |
| **Operating SOP Development** | Temporary | $(20/160 \times 70\text{k}) + (100/160 \times 120\text{k})$ | 83,750 |
| **Barista Payroll (2 FTE)** | Permanent | $2 \times 60,000\text{ RUB/mo}$ | 120,000 |
| **Location Manager Payroll** | Permanent | $1 \times 75,000\text{ RUB/mo}$ | 75,000 |
| **Coffee Beans Inventory** | Consumable | Initial stock purchase | 30,000 |
| **Operational Consumables** | Consumable | Cups, lids, syrups, cleaning supplies | 45,000 |
| **Premises Rent & Utilities** | Supporting | Monthly lease commitment | 70,000 |
| **Interior Renovation** | Supporting | Fit-out & preparation | 150,000 |
| **Online POS Cash Register** | Supporting | Hardware & fiscal module | 50,000 |
| **Launch Advertising Campaign** | Supporting | Local acquisition & promo | 80,000 |

**Feasibility Verdict:** With total initial outlays of **773,750 RUB** against projected monthly revenue of **500,000 RUB**, gross revenue payback occurs in **~1.55 months** ($773.75 / 500 = 1.5475$). Ongoing monthly operating expenses (rent, payroll, and consumables) total **340,000 RUB**, leaving **160,000 RUB/month** in operating contribution—making expansion financially viable provided sales volume stabilizes at target levels.

## 03. Client 2: Piterets Retail Network (Cashier Staffing Optimization)

* **Temporary:** Cashier workload audit & reporting (40 hrs business analyst); optimal shift-scheduling algorithm design (25 hrs business analyst).
* **Permanent:** Additional frontline cashiers (2 FTE @ 45,000 RUB/mo).
* **Material (Governing & Consumables):** Shift-scheduling model (embedded in analyst hours); spare cashier uniforms (15,000 RUB).
