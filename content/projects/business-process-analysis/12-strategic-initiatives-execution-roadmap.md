## 01. Strategic Objective & Transformation Scope

To eliminate inventory mis-sorting, raw material stockouts, and unplanned production line stoppages at **Cherepovetskie Paints**, the diagnostic findings were translated into a unified **Strategic Transformation Roadmap**. Rather than treating warehouse friction in isolation, the roadmap coordinates five cross-functional initiatives spanning **IT**, **Logistics**, **HR**, **Warehouse Operations**, and **Quality Assurance** over a **4-month execution horizon**.

---

## 02. Strategic Initiatives Matrix

| # | Strategic Initiative | Root Bottleneck Addressed | Target Outcome & KPIs | Timeline | Accountable Owner | Operational Segment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Warehouse Process Automation (WMS)** | Frequent material accounting errors causing inventory mis-sorting and stockouts | **-90%** accounting errors; real-time stock visibility and data accuracy | **3 Months** | Head of IT Department | Warehouse Logistics |
| **02** | **Supply Chain & Multi-Supplier Optimization** | Production line stoppages triggered by delayed raw material deliveries | **-50%** production downtime; uninterrupted raw material availability | **4 Months** | Logistics Manager | Logistics & Procurement |
| **03** | **Staff Training & SOP Standardization** | Insufficient warehouse staff qualification causing handling and recording errors | **-70%** operational errors; standardized workflows and higher shift throughput | **2 Months** | Head of HR Department | Human Resource Management |
| **04** | **Warehouse Space Reorganization** | Suboptimal floor layout slowing down material search and retrieval | **-60%** material search/picking time; streamlined floor navigation | **3 Months** | Warehouse Operations Manager | Warehouse Logistics |
| **05** | **Incoming Material Quality Control (IQC)** | Defective raw material batches causing line stoppages and finished-goods scrap | **-50%** manufacturing defect rate; consistent finished-product quality | **4 Months** | Head of Quality Department | Quality Management |
