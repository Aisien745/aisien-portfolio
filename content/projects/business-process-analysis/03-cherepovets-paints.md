## 01. Overview & Production Context

The **Cherepovetskie Paints** manufacturing plant faces severe internal supply chain and warehouse management friction. The analysis focuses on:
* **Inventory Audit BPMN Workflow (A4):** Mapping monthly stock verification cycles executed by warehouse keepers (ERP stock reconciliation, physical counting, discrepancy logging, and quality reporting sent to the Quality Director).
* **Problem Field Identification:** Examining market seasonality, raw material import sanctions, warehouse disorganization, and internal resistance to change.
* **Standardization & Training Framework:** Implementing corporate portal documentation, compliance testing, and training metrics.

---

## 02. Problem Field Diagnosis

### 1. Market & Supply Constraints
* **High Seasonality Impact:** Extreme demand swings distorting production schedules and finished goods inventory holding costs.
* **Raw Material Sanctions:** Severe import restrictions on key chemical components required for paint formulations.

### 2. Operational & Process Failures
* **Unclear Responsibility Zones:** Ambiguous ownership of inventory checkpoints leading to finger-pointing and accountability gaps.
* **Warehouse Disorganization ("Warehouse Chaos"):** Poor bin location mapping and physical clutter resulting in picking errors and prolonged inventory audit cycles.

### 3. Human Resources & Change Resistance
* **Employee Dissatisfaction:** Resistance and cultural friction arising from newly introduced warehouse restructuring and inventory protocols.

---

## 03. Solution Architecture & Implementation

To resolve industrial bottlenecks, the remediation framework focuses on two key pillars:

### 1. Process Documentation & Governance
* **Comprehensive SOP Repository:** Publishing all finished goods warehouse process regulations on the corporate portal.
* **Automated Compliance Testing:** Deploying mandatory digital knowledge-check tests for production and warehouse personnel linked to each regulation.

### 2. Workforce Training & Competency Standards
* **Mandatory Staff Upskilling:** 100% participation requirement for production staff in new warehouse handling workflows.
* **Performance Threshold:** Enforcing a minimum average test score of **≥95%** to certify operational readiness.

### Strategic Impact
Rebuilding warehouse governance eliminates search time, reduces inventory shrinkage, mitigates employee friction through structured training, and ensures stable manufacturing output despite seasonal volatility.
