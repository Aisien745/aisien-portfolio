## 01. Diagnostic Scope & Problem Field Methodology

Following stakeholder interviews at **Cherepovetskie Paints**, seven recurring operational and commercial pain points were cataloged and mapped into a four-domain **Problem Field** (**Finance**, **Market**, **Processes**, and **People**). Rather than treating symptoms in isolation, causal links were traced across domains to identify high-leverage root problems—where resolving a single upstream bottleneck eliminates multiple downstream failures.

---

## 02. Domain Classification of Identified Bottlenecks

| # | Identified Bottleneck | Problem Domain | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | Lack of a structured process to communicate product value to customers | **Market** | Weakens brand differentiation and price realization in competitive segments. |
| **02** | Missing or severely delayed customer feedback during order disputes | **Processes** | Erodes client trust, increases churn risk, and escalates complaint handling. |
| **03** | High frontline and shop-floor staff turnover | **People** | Depletes institutional knowledge and lowers shift productivity. |
| **04** | Outdated or missing documentation for core operating processes | **Processes** | Forces reliance on oral handoffs, driving inconsistent execution and rework. |
| **05** | Raw material procurement failures (unclear reorder lead-time triggers) | **Processes** | Triggers unexpected stockouts and costly production line stoppages. |
| **06** | Heavy debt obligations incurred to finance CIS market expansion | **Finance** | Tightens working-capital liquidity and leaves minimal margin for operational delays. |
| **07** | Chronic employee overtime across production and warehouse shifts | **People** | Accelerates workforce burnout, error rates, and attrition. |
