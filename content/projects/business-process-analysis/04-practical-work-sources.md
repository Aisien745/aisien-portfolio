## 01. Overview & Methodology

Effective business process reengineering requires rigorous information gathering. When analyzing organizational bottlenecks across retail, hospitality, and manufacturing, we rely on a structured mix of normative documentation, internal/external domain experts, and industry literature. 

---

## 02. Client 1: Casual Coffee Shop (Strategy: Improvement)

To optimize existing workflows and quality control standards in the absence of a dedicated quality manager, we utilize the following sources:

* **Normative Documentation**
  * *Federal Law No. 54-FZ (KKT Regulation):* Ensures cash register and settlement compliance.
  * *Law No. 2300-1 (Consumer Protection):* Guides customer service adjustments and dispute handling.
  * *Examples:* Auditing cash register equipment compliance; updating return and exchange policies.
* **Domain Experts**
  * *Internal:* Coffee shop owner, possessing deep operational knowledge of day-to-day constraints.
  * *External:* HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point) food safety standards specialist.
  * *Examples:* Conducting internal HACCP training sessions; collaborating on new food safety control procedures.
* **Literature & Publications**
  * *The Ideal Coffee Shop* by J. Talbot and current specialty coffee industry trend reports.
  * *Examples:* Implementing best-practice management frameworks; adjusting product assortment based on market trends.

## 03. Client 2: Piterets Retail Network (Strategy: Improvement)

Focusing directly on cashier staffing optimization, queue reduction, and checkout efficiency:

* **Normative Documentation**
  * *Federal Law No. 381-FZ (Trade Activity Regulation) & SanPiN 2.3/2.4.3590-20:* Ensures sanitary and regulatory compliance in retail environments.
  * *Examples:* Auditing store operations against trade regulations; implementing updated sanitary protocols.
* **Domain Experts**
  * *Internal:* Logistics manager, overseeing stock flow and store-level resource allocation.
  * *External:* Inventory management specialist.
  * *Examples:* Running internal logistics seminars; consulting on advanced inventory turnover methods.
* **Literature & Publications**
  * *Supply Chain Management* by R. Handfield and retail technology adoption papers.
  * *Examples:* Applying modern supply chain frameworks; evaluating automated retail tech solutions.

## 04. Client 3: Cherepovetskie Paints (Strategy: Reengineering)

Targeting warehouse chaos, inventory accounting, and manufacturing workflow reengineering:

* **Normative Documentation**
  * *GOST 25137-82 (Chemical Resistance Testing) & TR CU 005/2011 (Packaging Safety).*
  * *Examples:* Designing new product testing methodologies; enforcing packaging safety standards.
* **Domain Experts**
  * *Internal:* Chief Technologist, directing core production and formulation standards.
  * *External:* Chemical safety consultant.
  * *Examples:* Co-authoring new production standards; integrating advanced safety protocols.
* **Literature & Publications**
  * *Chemistry and Technology of Paint Materials* by A. Zhukov and sustainable coating journals.
  * *Examples:* Upgrading production techniques; developing eco-friendly product lines.
