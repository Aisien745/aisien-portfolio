## 01. Risk Framework & Methodology

Process optimization and expansion introduce both downside vulnerabilities (delays, quality drops, compliance breaches) and upside operational risks (demand surges exceeding baseline capacity). Each risk is evaluated by **Likelihood** and **Impact**, paired with a targeted response strategy (**Avoidance**, **Mitigation**, or **Acceptance**) and concrete preventive controls.

## 02. Client 1: Casual Coffee Shop (Expansion & Training)

| Risk Event | Source | Likelihood | Impact | Strategy | Mitigation & Response Plan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Difficulty securing three suitable locations** | Owner | High | High | Avoidance | Target expandable commercial spaces with long-term lease locks; keep renovation or modular build-outs as fallback options. |
| **Locations scattered across opposite ends of the city** | Administrator | Medium | Medium | Mitigation | Restrict site selection to central, high-transit districts; delegate supply runs to delivery services or hire a regional supervisor. |
| **Excessive foot traffic exceeding service capacity** *(Upside Risk)* | Marketer | Medium | High | Acceptance | Hire flexible peak-hour part-time staff and automate repetitive bar preparation steps to protect throughput. |
| **Lack of dedicated training space for new hires** | Barista | High | Medium | Mitigation | Establish a dedicated off-site training station, schedule practical drills during off-hours, and move theory to online modules. |
| **Customer dissatisfaction from live-shift trainee errors** | Barista | Medium | High | Avoidance | Conduct initial certification in training centers and onboard new baristas gradually onto low-complexity tasks. |
| **Equipment shortages during new store launches** | Operational Audit | Medium | Medium | Acceptance | Pre-order buffer espresso and kitchen equipment ahead of launch and secure rapid-replacement vendor SLAs. |
| **Ingredient supply chain disruptions** | Operational Audit | Low | High | Mitigation | Maintain multi-supplier contracts for core coffee and dairy inputs alongside safety-stock buffers. |

## 03. Client 2: Piterets Retail Network (Checkout & Staffing)

| Risk Event | Source | Likelihood | Impact | Strategy | Mitigation & Response Plan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overstaffing cashiers per shift eroding ROI** | Network Head | Low | Medium | Acceptance | Optimize shift schedules dynamically using hourly checkout traffic and queue-length analytics. |
| **Inexperienced hires slowing down checkout queues** | Operations Director | High | High | Mitigation | Implement structured onboarding bootcamps, peer mentorship, and regular speed/accuracy evaluations. |
| **Missing mandatory medical clearances delaying onboarding** | Senior Cashier | Medium | High | Avoidance | Enforce valid medical health certificates as a strict pre-employment prerequisite before shift assignment. |
| **Outdated customer service SOPs** | Methodologist | Medium | Medium | Mitigation | Audit and update service manuals continuously via frontline feedback and host them in a searchable knowledge base. |
| **Senior cashiers losing productive time training recruits** | Methodologist | High | Medium | Mitigation | Standardize onboarding with self-paced video tutorials and digital simulations to reduce 1-on-1 shadowing hours. |
| **POS terminal and cash register hardware failures** | Operational Audit | Medium | High | Mitigation | Run preventive hardware maintenance schedules and keep hot-swappable backup terminals on-site. |
| **High frontline cashier turnover** | Operational Audit | Medium | High | Mitigation | Introduce performance-linked retention bonuses, clearer shift predictability, and regular manager check-ins. |

## 04. Client 3: Cherepovetskie Paints (Warehouse & Compliance)

| Risk Event | Source | Likelihood | Impact | Strategy | Mitigation & Response Plan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shipment mix-ups between Luxury and Standard product lines** | Shop Floor Head | Medium | High | Mitigation | Enforce segregated dispatch rules paired with high-contrast, color-coded SKU and pallet labeling. |
| **Overly complex, unreadable operating SOPs** | Shop Floor Head | Medium | Medium | Mitigation | Rewrite technical instructions into visual step-by-step diagrams and conduct hands-on floor briefings. |
| **Lack of computer access for shop-floor staff to view digital SOPs** | Shift Supervisor | High | Medium | Mitigation | Deploy laminated print SOPs directly at workstations and install shared self-service kiosk terminals on the floor. |
| **Cheating and credential sharing during staff testing** | HR Manager | Medium | Medium | Avoidance | Generate randomized, unique test variants per employee and enforce individual ID authentication at login. |
| **Shortage of qualified technical production staff** | Operational Audit | Medium | High | Acceptance | Run continuous internal upskilling tracks and establish talent pipelines with local technical colleges. |
| **Manufacturing equipment breakdown** | Operational Audit | Medium | High | Mitigation | Enforce preventive maintenance schedules and maintain an on-site inventory of critical spare parts. |
| **Raw material delivery delays** | Operational Audit | Low | High | Acceptance | Diversify chemical and packaging suppliers and maintain strategic reserve buffers for long-lead inputs. |
