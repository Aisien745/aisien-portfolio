## What is GDP?

**Gross Domestic Product** is the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a given period.

$$
Y = C + I + G + (X - M)
$$

| Component | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| C | Household consumption |
| I | Investment by firms |
| G | Government spending |
| X − M | Net exports |

## Nominal vs real GDP

Nominal GDP uses current prices, real GDP uses constant prices. Growth rates only make sense in **real** terms.

> Key idea: always ask whether a number is nominal or real before comparing it over time.

## What drives long-run growth?

1. Capital accumulation
2. Labour force and human capital
3. Technological progress (the residual that explains most of the difference)
