## Measuring inflation

Inflation is the percentage change in the price level, usually measured with the CPI.

$$
\pi_t = \frac{P_t - P_{t-1}}{P_{t-1}} \times 100
$$

## Causes

- **Demand-pull:** too much money chasing too few goods
- **Cost-push:** rising input costs such as energy or wages
- **Expectations:** if everyone expects higher prices, they set them

## The Phillips curve

Short-run trade-off between inflation and unemployment, which breaks down when expectations adjust.
