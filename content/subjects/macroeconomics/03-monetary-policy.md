## The toolkit

1. Policy interest rate
2. Open market operations / asset purchases
3. Reserve requirements and forward guidance

## Transmission

A rate change moves through borrowing costs, asset prices, the exchange rate and expectations, and ends up in demand and inflation.

## The ECB

The European Central Bank targets medium-term inflation of 2%.
